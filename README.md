ZY10A uses two sensors to follow a line made from black duct tape.

- If neither sensor detects black, the car moves straight.
- If the left sensor detects black, the left wheel slows down and the car turns left.
- If the right sensor detects black, the right wheel slows down and the car turns right.
- Both wheels continue moving forward while the car turns.

LO 11/13
1. Identify the main parts of the car.
2. Assemble the car by following the video tutorial.
3. Connect the motors, battery, and sensors.
4. Test the car on a black-tape track.
   
Always double check wiring before turning on power. Raise the wheels off the table during the first test.
